Meet Yuga, the voice AI agent that turns merchant conversations into business actions.

Yuga answers a merchant’s phone line, looks up real catalogue and customer records, and can draft an invoice after the caller confirms. It is not a FAQ bot. It is an operations agent on a live call.

Live call path
Customer phone→ Exotel→ Yuga→ Inventory→ Invoice

Spoken replies never include Mongo IDs. Tools keep the exact product and customer IDs for the database.

The problem

Phone orders still mean manual work.

Before

  • Repeat availability and price questions.
  • Open inventory to check stock.
  • Look up or create the customer by hand.
  • Write the order, then raise an invoice later.
  • A missed call is often a missed order.

With Yuga

  • The caller speaks naturally on the merchant number.
  • Stock and price come from the live catalogue.
  • The incoming number links or creates the customer.
  • A confirmed quote can become a draft invoice.
  • The merchant reviews the result in the existing billing app.

No market-size claims. This is the operational loop the current stack is built to shorten.

Capabilities

What is in the codebase today.

Working

Voice calls

Exotel inbound audio, Silero VAD, Nemotron ASR, Kokoro TTS, hang-up after goodbye or invoice.

Working

Catalogue

search_catalog lists or searches Mongo inventory. Returns name, price, unit, stock, and item_id.

Working

Customers

Incoming number lookup on customerdetails. Creates an Individual customer if none exists.

Working

Quotes & invoices

quote_order totals from the database. After an explicit yes, create_invoice writes a draft. Duplicates are blocked.

No invented middleware. TTS and ASR stay local in both inference modes. Only the LLM host changes.

INFERENCE_MODE

Same operations. Two inference hosts.

We did not reuse NODE_ENV. That already means development or production in Node. The switch is INFERENCE_MODE.

local

Merchant box

llama-server on :8081, Qwen3-0.6B. Nemo speech on :8080. Kokoro TTS in-process. If the local LLM is down, the call errors. It does not fall back to Groq.

provider · default

Hosted LLM

Groq openai/gpt-oss-120b at https://api.groq.com/openai/v1. Needs GROQ_API_KEY or LLM_API_KEY. ASR and TTS stay local.

Required: INFERENCE_MODE=local|provider · GROQ_API_KEY or LLM_API_KEY (provider) · optional LLM_MODEL, LLM_BASE_URL, ASR_URL. Catalogue, customers, and invoices always use Mongo. They do not change with the model host.
